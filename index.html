Nutrition tips
01
Hydration

Drink Before You're Thirsty

Aim for 30–35ml per kg of bodyweight daily. Add an extra 500ml for every hour of exercise. Start your morning with a big glass of water before coffee.

02
Protein

Protein at Every Meal

Include a quality protein source at each meal — eggs, chicken, fish, legumes, or Greek yogurt. This supports muscle recovery and keeps you fuller longer.

03
Timing

Pre-Workout Fuel

Eat a balanced meal 90–120 minutes before training. Aim for carbs + protein + small fat. A banana with peanut butter works perfectly in a pinch.

04
Recovery

Post-Workout Window

Within 30–60 minutes after training, have protein + fast carbs. This kickstarts muscle repair. A protein shake with fruit is simple and effective.

Mindset tips
01
Focus

The 5-Minute Rule

On days you don't want to train — commit to just 5 minutes. Most of the time you'll keep going. Starting is the hardest part.

02
Stress

Box Breathing

Inhale 4 counts → Hold 4 → Exhale 4 → Hold 4. Repeat 4 times. Use before a workout, stressful meeting, or whenever you feel overwhelmed.

03
Habits

Stack Your Habits

Attach new habits to existing ones. After I make coffee → I do 5 minutes of stretching. The anchor makes the habit stick much faster.

04
Sleep

Sleep is Training

70% of muscle repair happens during sleep. 7–9 hours isn't a luxury — it's part of your training plan. Protect it like a workout session.

05
Identity

Become the Person

Don't say "I'm trying to get fit." Say "I'm someone who moves every day." Identity-based habits are the most powerful form of lasting change.
